# Polyglot Persistence over a Shared Database

## Status: Active

## Date: 2026-02-10

## Owner: Jhon

## Context: Sharing a single database across microservices causes schema conflicts and bottlenecks.

## Decision: Enforce a "database-per-service" pattern. Each microservice owns its data store.

## Consequences:
- Positive: Complete independence in deploying and scaling services.
- Negative: Distributed transactions become harder; eventual consistency must be managed.
