# Product variations storage

## Owner: Jhon

## Context: Product could have small variations that final clients may specify when a sale is done, and then requiring to provide this product variation at checkout.

## Decision: Stock will not register product variations so final client can not be specified this details in the sell order.

## Alternatives considered:
- Separate as a diferent product each variation
- Create a dynamic one-to-many relation with a generic record specifing variation characteristics.

## Consequences:
- Positive: Simplifies the stock managment, checkout and selling process
- Negative: Clients can not specify product variations
