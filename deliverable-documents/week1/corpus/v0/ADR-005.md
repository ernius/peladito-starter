# Product price persistance

## Owner: Jhon

## Context: Product has price as its properties

## Decision: Product price is stored in "product" table

## Alternatives considered:
- Create a many-to-many relation with warehouse so to support different prices per warehouse.

## Consequences:
- Positive: Simplifies the stock managment and selling process
- Negative: Sellers can not modify prices
